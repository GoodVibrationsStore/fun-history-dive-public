# Clue: [short name]

- **Question:** What are you trying to find out?
- **Observation:** What did you notice?
- **Personal meaning:** What connection or interpretation came to mind?
- **Source:** Who wrote or recorded it, where can it be found, and when?
- **Evidence:** What does the source actually say? Include a passage or reference.
- **Interpretation:** What might it mean, and what is uncertain?
- **Alternatives:** What other explanations could fit?
- **Next step:** What source or question should you check next?
- **Status:** Open / supported / challenged / unresolved

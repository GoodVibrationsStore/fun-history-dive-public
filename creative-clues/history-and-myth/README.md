# History and Myth: The Shared Case Board

This folder brings the two sides of the investigation together: the
historical sources and family traditions, and the mythic possibilities
they suggest for the book. A clue can be interesting without being solved.

## Open case files

1. [The maternal branches and the rose line](./maternal-branches-and-rose-line.md):
   Mary, Anne, Elizabeth, Sobe, their proposed relatives, and the question
   of a continuous maternal thread.
2. [Missing daughters and the disputed inheritance](./missing-daughters-and-inheritance.md):
   unnamed women, Jubilees' named daughters, women seeking inheritance,
   John and Jesus, and the possibility of a hidden branch or switched role.
3. [The cosmic family and the anchor](./cosmic-family-and-anchor.md):
   Sophia, divine counterparts, the Rebis, repeated ages, the maternal
   seed, and nested family stories as possible story mechanisms.
4. [Medusa, Athena, and the sisters](./medusa-athena-and-the-sisters.md):
   Ceto's daughters, Medusa's children, Athena's mother, and contrasting
   ancient accounts of images and guardians.
5. [Ishi, Gomer, and the children](./ishi-gomer-and-the-children.md):
   a prophetic name clue, a named daughter, and the limits of the
   associated maternal branch.

Each file keeps three things visible together:

- **Source clue:** what a specific text, tradition, or scientific principle
  actually says, including where its account stops.
- **Mythic possibility:** what the book could make of that clue, including
  possibilities still undecided.
- **Case question:** what to look for next, or what choice remains open.

An ancient text is evidence of a story or belief, not automatically a
firsthand family record. Later versions can contradict one another. A
creative connection is welcome even when no historical link has been found;
labeling the difference preserves both the mystery and the research.

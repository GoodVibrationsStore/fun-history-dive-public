# Israelite Religion Before Jesus

**Question:** What changed in religious traditions before Jesus, and what
could count as evidence for a particular change?

## A historical lead, not a solved takeover

Israelite and Judahite religion developed over centuries in conversation
with neighboring cultures. In *The Early History of God*, scholar Mark S.
Smith argues for processes of convergence and differentiation involving
Yahweh, El, Baal, and Asherah. This is a scholarly reconstruction from
multiple kinds of evidence, not proof of a single secret replacement event.
See Richard Hess's [review of Smith's book](https://denverjournal.denverseminary.edu/the-denver-journal-article/the-early-history-of-god/);
the review also questions parts of the reconstruction.

## Passages to compare

- [Deuteronomy 6:4–5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Deuteronomy%206%3A4-5&version=NRSVUE):
  a statement central to Jewish worship. Compare translations and
  interpretations rather than treating one English wording as decisive.
- [Psalm 82](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Psalm%2082&version=NRSVUE):
  a divine-council scene. Scholars debate how such language relates to
  ancient Israelite beliefs; the poem alone does not reconstruct the whole
  history of worship.
- [2 Kings 23:4–7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2%20Kings%2023%3A4-7&version=NRSVUE):
  a narrative of Josiah's removal of cult objects. It presents events from
  the biblical writer's perspective; compare it with archaeological evidence
  and scholarly discussion before reconstructing what happened.

The dates of events described, the writing and editing of texts, and the
surviving manuscripts are different questions. Changes in worship and
interpretation do not by themselves prove that Jesus's family was replaced
or that any particular person's teachings were stolen.

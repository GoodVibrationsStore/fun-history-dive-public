# Starter Leads

These are questions to investigate, not conclusions. "Jesus's line" might mean
his family lineage, a line of teachings, or both.

## Family lineage

- **Clue:** Matthew 1:1–16 and Luke 3:23–38 give different genealogies of Jesus.
- **Question:** Where do the lists differ, and how have historians and religious
  interpreters explained those differences?
- **Limit:** A genealogy in a text is a claim made by its author; it does not
  by itself establish biological ancestry or prove deliberate alteration.

## Before Jesus

- **Clue:** Later Christian writers connected Jesus with earlier Jewish texts,
  including 2 Samuel 7:12–16 and Isaiah 53.
- **Question:** What did these passages mean in their earlier contexts, and how
  did later readers interpret them?
- **Limit:** Later interpretations should not automatically be treated as the
  original authors' intentions.

## Teachings and communities

- **Clue:** Paul describes disagreements with other believers in Galatians
  2:11–14; the Gospel of Thomas contains sayings attributed to Jesus.
- **Question:** What kinds of disagreement existed among early followers, and
  how do different texts portray Jesus and his message?
- **Limit:** Disagreement or difference alone does not establish a coordinated
  takeover. Check the date, authorship, and context of each source.

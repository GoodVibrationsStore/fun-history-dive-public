# Lineage and the Rose: Open Leads

**Question:** What is meant by "the rose line," and what can actual sources
show about Jesus's ancestry and any claimed descendants?

## Jesus's ancestry in two texts

Compare [Matthew 1:1–16 and Luke 3:23–38](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew%201%3A1-16%3BLuke%203%3A23-38&version=NRSVUE):

| Question | Matthew | Luke |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direction | Abraham toward Jesus | Jesus back toward Adam |
| David's son in the list | Solomon | Nathan |
| Joseph's father in the list | Jacob | Heli |

Both genealogies connect Jesus to David and Abraham, and both name Joseph.
Interpreters have proposed different ways of reconciling the lists, including
legal relationships and a maternal genealogy. Luke does not explicitly call
his list Mary's genealogy. The differences require explanation, but neither a
deliberate alteration nor a complete biological pedigree follows from them.

## The rose: keep meanings separate

The earlier conversation's intended meaning is not visible yet. Possible
avenues to distinguish, not assumptions about that discussion:

1. **A symbolic or imagined maternal line:** a creative or spiritual motif;
   investigate its meaning separately from a documented family tree.
2. **A claimed Jesus–Mary Magdalene bloodline:** require an early source for
   each link, including the alleged marriage, child, and later descendants.
   The sources reviewed here do not establish such a chain.
3. **A geographic line in Paris:** the brass line at Saint-Sulpice is an
   astronomical instrument; a [description of the church](https://urbantales.net/paris/saint-sulpice.html)
   distinguishes it from the fictional account in *The Da Vinci Code*. Do not
   assume this is the same meaning as a family line.

The [NASSCAL Gospel of Mary entry](https://www.nasscal.com/e-clavis-christian-apocrypha/gospel-of-mary/)
describes the surviving text and notes its use in popular culture. Its
portrayal of Mary as an important disciple is not evidence that she and Jesus
were married or had children.

its headings. The snapshot previously used contains approximately 54,312
lines of browser output. It includes page markup and may contain collapsed
messages: reviewing its visible text does not prove that hidden or unloaded
content was available. All 648 visible user-message headings were mapped
earlier; **the complete messages and responses have not all been read**.

note when a collapsed or inaccessible section needs another method. Record
only themes and source links in this public repository, not personal family

# Anne: Mary's Proposed Mother

The [*Protevangelium of James*, chapters 1–5](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0847.htm)
names Anna and Joachim as Mary's parents. It does not name Anna's mother.

| Source | Children attributed to Anne | Maternal-line consequence if biological |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Protevangelium* | Mary, mother of Jesus | Mary and Jesus would ordinarily carry Anne's mtDNA. |
| [*Golden Legend*](https://www.christianiconography.info/goldenLegend/maryNativity.htm) | Mary mother of Jesus; Mary Cleophas; [Mary Salome](../mary-salome/notes.md) | All three daughters and their children would carry Anne's maternal line despite the legend giving them different fathers. |
| [Emmerich's vision](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/emmerich/lifemary.v.html) | An elder daughter named Mary by Joachim, and later Mary mother of Jesus | The sisters would ordinarily share Anne's mtDNA. The text is a nineteenth-century visionary account, not first-century evidence. |

**Proposed mother and sisters:** See [Ismeria](../ismeria/notes.md),
[Sobe](../sobe/notes.md), and [Maraha](../maraha/notes.md). These
relationships vary by source and must not be joined automatically.

# Rhoda and Mara

In [Emmerich's visionary account](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/emmerich/lifemary.v.html),
Rhoda is a daughter of [Emerentia](../emerentia/notes.md). One of Rhoda's
daughters is **Mara**, described as present at Mary's death.

```text
Emorun → Emerentia → Rhoda → Mara
```

The account also says Rhoda had other daughters but does not name them in
this passage. These are potential female-line branches only within the
visionary tree; no independently verified ancient mtDNA is established.

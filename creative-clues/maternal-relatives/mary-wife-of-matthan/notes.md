# Mary, Wife of Matthan

In a [secondary quotation of a Byzantine family tradition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sobe_(sister_of_Saint_Anne)),
Mary wife of Matthan has three daughters: [elder Mary](../elder-mary/notes.md),
[Sobe](../sobe/notes.md), and [Anne](../anne/notes.md). The reported
branches continue through Salome the midwife, Elizabeth and John the
Baptist, and Mary mother of Jesus and Jesus.

If all these mother–child relationships were biological, the people shown
would ordinarily inherit this Mary's maternal line. The original Greek
and its transmission still need checking; neither her own mother nor a
genetic profile is established here. Do not substitute Emerentia as her
name or mix this tree with the conflicting Emmerich account.

# Enue: Daughter of Emerentia

In [Emmerich's visionary account](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/emmerich/lifemary.v.html),
Enue is a daughter of [Emerentia](../emerentia/notes.md) and sister of
Elizabeth and Rhoda. She was named after her aunt
[Enue, daughter of Emorun](../enue-daughter-of-emorun/notes.md).

If the proposed links were biological, she would ordinarily carry
Emerentia's maternal line. The cited passage does not name this Enue's
children, so the branch stops here pending further sources.

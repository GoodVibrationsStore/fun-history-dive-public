# Ismeria / Hismeria: Two Positions in the Tree

| Account | Relationship to Anne | Children attributed in that account |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [*Golden Legend*](https://www.christianiconography.info/goldenLegend/maryNativity.htm) | Hismeria is Anne's sister. Their mother is not named. | [Elizabeth](../elizabeth/notes.md), and Eliud. The Caxton translation calls Eliud a daughter, while a [painting's interpretation](https://www.christianiconography.info/Wikimedia%20Commons/holyKinship.html) calls Eliud a son; check the textual variants before tracing further mtDNA. |
| [Emmerich's vision](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/emmerich/lifemary.v.html) | Ismeria is Anne's mother and Emorun's daughter. | [Sobe](../sobe/notes.md), [Anne](../anne/notes.md), [Maraha](../maraha/notes.md), an unnamed sister, and other unnamed siblings. |

The same or similar name does not resolve these conflicts. In the visionary
version, daughters of Ismeria would ordinarily pass her mtDNA to their
children, while sons would ordinarily not do so.

The *Golden Legend* continues its Eliud branch through **Eminen** and
**Servatius**. Since the sources differ on Eliud's sex and the exact
relationships need checking, this cannot yet be treated as an established
unbroken female-line path. The visionary account names an Eliud in a
different position in the tree: [Sobe's son](../sobe/notes.md).

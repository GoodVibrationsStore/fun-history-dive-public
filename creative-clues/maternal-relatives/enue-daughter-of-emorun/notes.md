# Enue: Daughter of Emorun

In [Emmerich's visionary account](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/emmerich/lifemary.v.html),
Enue is a daughter of [Emorun](../emorun/notes.md) and sister of Ismeria
and Emerentia. The passage says a descendant of hers was with Jesus, but
does not identify the intervening mother–child links. Her children are not
named there; the mtDNA branch cannot be carried further on that evidence.

She is distinct from [Enue, Emerentia's daughter](../enue-daughter-of-emerentia/notes.md),
whom the account says was named after her.

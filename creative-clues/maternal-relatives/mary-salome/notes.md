# Mary Salome and the Sons of Thunder

[Mark 3:17](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark%203%3A17&version=NRSVUE)
calls **James and John, sons of Zebedee**, “Sons of Thunder.” It does not
say they were twins. They are not Romulus and Remus; those are the legendary
Roman twins born to Rhea Silvia in [Roman tradition](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Romulus-and-Remus).

| Account | Proposed mother of Mary Salome | Her sons in that account |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [*Golden Legend*](https://www.christianiconography.info/goldenLegend/maryNativity.htm) | [Anne](../anne/notes.md), making her a half-sister of Mary mother of Jesus | James the Greater and John the Evangelist, sons of Zebedee. |
| [Emmerich's vision](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/emmerich/lifemary.v.html) | [Sobe](../sobe/notes.md), making her a niece of Anne | James and John, sons of Zebedee. |

If either proposed maternal chain were biologically accurate, the brothers
would ordinarily inherit their mother's mtDNA. Neither account establishes
their descendants through an uninterrupted female line. John son of
Zebedee is a different person from [John the Baptist](../elizabeth/notes.md).
The Salome described as a midwife in another tradition is not automatically
the same woman.

## A later text's John

The opening of the [*Apocryphon of John*](https://earlychristianwritings.com/text/apocryphonjohn.html)
identifies its speaking character John as **the brother of James**, with
both described as sons of Zebedee. This is a useful link to the Sons of
Thunder in the text's own framing—not John the Baptist, Elizabeth's son.
The framing does not establish that the historical John wrote the work,
or identify his mother or a verified genetic relationship to Jesus.

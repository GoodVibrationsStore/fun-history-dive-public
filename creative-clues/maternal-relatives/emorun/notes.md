# Emorun / Moruni: Visionary Ancestor

The nineteenth-century [visionary account recorded by Brentano](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/emmerich/lifemary.v.html)
names Emorun as mother of:

- **[Ismeria](../ismeria/notes.md)** → Sobe, Anne, Maraha, and other siblings.
- **[Emerentia](../emerentia/notes.md)** → Elizabeth, Enue, and Rhoda.
- **Enue**, a different person from Emerentia's daughter of the same name.
  The account says one of her descendants was with Jesus, without giving
  an uninterrupted maternal chain to that person in the passage cited.

In this story Emorun would be Anne's maternal grandmother. The account does
not identify Emorun's mother or supply independently verified ancient
records. A maternal-line diagram is therefore a conditional reading of the
vision, not a documented path to a primordial ancestor.

# Elizabeth: Mother of John the Baptist

In [Luke 1:5–7, 36, 57–66](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke%201%3A5-7%2C36%2C57-66&version=NRSVUE),
Elizabeth is Mary's relative and the mother of **John the Baptist**.
Luke does not identify Elizabeth's mother, her precise relation to Mary,
or additional children.

| Account | Proposed mother | Connection to Mary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Byzantine quotation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sobe_(sister_of_Saint_Anne)) | [Sobe](../sobe/notes.md) | Elizabeth and Mary are daughters of sisters. |
| [*Golden Legend*](https://www.christianiconography.info/goldenLegend/maryNativity.htm) | [Hismeria](../ismeria/notes.md) | Elizabeth and Mary are daughters of sisters. |
| [Emmerich](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/emmerich/lifemary.v.html) | [Emerentia](../emerentia/notes.md) | Elizabeth and Mary's grandmother Ismeria are daughters of Emorun. |

Each proposed connection is conditional, not a confirmed mtDNA match.
John the Baptist is **not** John son of Zebedee, one of the Sons of
Thunder. The sources cited here do not establish another child of
Elizabeth or maternal-line descendants through John.

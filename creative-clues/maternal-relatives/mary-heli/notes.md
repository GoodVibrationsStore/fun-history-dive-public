# Mary Heli: Anne's Elder Daughter in Emmerich's Account

The [nineteenth-century visionary account](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/emmerich/lifemary.v.html)
describes **Mary Heli** as the first daughter of Anne and Joachim and as
an older sister of Mary, mother of Jesus. If biological, the sisters would
ordinarily share their mother Anne's mtDNA.

This is not the elder Mary who is Anne's sister in the
[Byzantine tree](../elder-mary/notes.md). The present branch map does not
yet establish a sourced list of Mary Heli's children; check the relevant
passage before extending the branch.

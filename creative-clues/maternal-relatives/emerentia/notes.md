# Emerentia: Conflicting Maternal Placements

In a tradition reported by the compiler in [Emmerich's chapter 1, note 16](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/emmerich/lifemary.v.html),
Emerentia is the mother of **Anne** and **Ismeria**, and Ismeria is
Elizabeth's mother. The original source for this particular reported tree
remains to be identified.

In the main visionary account in the same chapter, Emerentia is instead
**Ismeria's sister** and a daughter of [Emorun](../emorun/notes.md). Her
children there are:

- **[Elizabeth](../elizabeth/notes.md)** → John the Baptist.
- **Enue**, named after Emerentia's sister Enue.
- **[Rhoda](../rhoda/notes.md)** → Mara.

These are separate versions. Under the visionary version's stated
mother–child links, both Elizabeth's and Rhoda's branches would carry
Emorun's maternal line if biologically accurate; the text does not establish
their DNA.

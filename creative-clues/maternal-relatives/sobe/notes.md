# Sobe: Two Different Family Branches

## Byzantine three-sister version

The [quoted later Byzantine tradition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sobe_(sister_of_Saint_Anne))
names Mary wife of Matthan as mother of three sisters: elder Mary, Sobe,
and Anne. It makes **Sobe the mother of [Elizabeth](../elizabeth/notes.md)**,
and Elizabeth the mother of John the Baptist.

```text
Mary wife of Matthan
├── Sobe → Elizabeth → John the Baptist
└── Anne → Mary mother of Jesus → Jesus
```

The source is a secondary quotation; its underlying Greek needs checking.
If these mother–child links were biological, both named sons would receive
the same maternal line through the sisters.

## Emmerich's different version

In [Emmerich's visionary account](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/emmerich/lifemary.v.html),
Sobe and Anne are daughters of Ismeria. Sobe's children are:

- **[Mary Salome](../mary-salome/notes.md)**, said to bear James and John,
  sons of Zebedee.
- An **unnamed daughter**, said to be mother of three disciples whose names
  are not supplied in that passage.
- A son, **Eliud**. The account identifies him as father of an unnamed boy
  raised by Jesus and husband of Maroni of Naim. He would ordinarily
  receive Sobe's mtDNA, but his son would not ordinarily inherit it from
  him. Do not assume he is the same as Hismeria's child Eliud in the
  *Golden Legend*.

In this version **Elizabeth is not Sobe's daughter**: she is the daughter
of [Emerentia](../emerentia/notes.md), Ismeria's sister. These alternatives
cannot be combined into one established pedigree.

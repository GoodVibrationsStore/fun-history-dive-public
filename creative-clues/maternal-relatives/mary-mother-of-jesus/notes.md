# Mary, Mother of Jesus

**Earliest lead:** [Luke 1:30–36](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke%201%3A30-36&version=NRSVUE)
names Mary as Jesus's mother and Elizabeth as Mary's relative. It does not
give the precise relationship or name Mary's mother.

**Children and branches:** [Mark 6:3](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark%206%3A3&version=NRSVUE)
names Jesus's brothers James, Joses, Judas, and Simon and refers to unnamed
sisters. Interpretations differ: some regard them as Mary's children, others
as step-siblings or other relatives. Only if a person was Mary's biological
child would that link ordinarily imply her mtDNA.

**Parent lead:** [Anne](../anne/notes.md) is named as Mary's mother in the
second-century *Protevangelium of James*, not in the Gospels.

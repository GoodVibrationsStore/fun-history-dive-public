# Mary Cleophas and Her Children

The medieval [*Golden Legend*](https://www.christianiconography.info/goldenLegend/maryNativity.htm)
names Mary Cleophas as a daughter of [Anne](../anne/notes.md) and her
second husband Cleophas. It says Mary Cleophas married Alpheus and bore
four sons: **James the Less, Joseph the Just, Simon, and Jude**.

In that legend she is a maternal half-sister of Mary, mother of Jesus,
and of [Mary Salome](../mary-salome/notes.md). If those mother–child
relationships were biological, all four sons would ordinarily carry
Anne's maternal mtDNA, but they would not ordinarily pass it to their
children. The legend does not establish a daughter through whom to trace
this branch onward.

This medieval genealogy is not proof of the relationships or an mtDNA
match; identification of names across different texts needs separate study.

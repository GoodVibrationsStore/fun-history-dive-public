# Maternal Relatives: Person-by-Person Leads

This folder follows Jesus's proposed maternal relatives and their children.
Each page separates what a source says from what would follow **if** its
relationships were biological. These are working research leads, not
verified DNA matches.

| Person | Why follow this branch |
| --- | --- |
| [Mary, mother of Jesus](./mary-mother-of-jesus/notes.md) | Starting point; her possible other children are disputed. |
| [Anne](./anne/notes.md) | Mary's mother in the *Protevangelium*; later traditions propose other daughters. |
| [Sobe](./sobe/notes.md) | Anne's sister in different trees, but her children differ between them. |
| [Elizabeth](./elizabeth/notes.md) | Mother of John the Baptist and Mary's relative in Luke. |
| [Mary Salome](./mary-salome/notes.md) | Later accounts connect her to James and John, the “Sons of Thunder.” |
| [Mary Cleophas](./mary-cleophas/notes.md) | Anne's daughter and mother of four sons in the medieval Holy Kinship. |
| [Mary Heli](./mary-heli/notes.md) | The elder daughter of Anne and Joachim in Emmerich's vision. |
| [Elder Mary](./elder-mary/notes.md) | In one Byzantine family tree, Anne's sister and Salome's mother. |
| [Mary, wife of Matthan](./mary-wife-of-matthan/notes.md) | Proposed mother of the three sisters in the Byzantine tree. |
| [Ismeria / Hismeria](./ismeria/notes.md) | Different traditions place her in different generations. |
| [Emerentia](./emerentia/notes.md) | Proposed maternal ancestor, aunt, or sister depending on the source. |
| [Emorun](./emorun/notes.md) | Older ancestor in Emmerich's nineteenth-century visionary tree. |
| [Enue, daughter of Emorun](./enue-daughter-of-emorun/notes.md) | A visionary branch whose maternal descendants remain unclear. |
| [Enue, daughter of Emerentia](./enue-daughter-of-emerentia/notes.md) | Her similarly named niece, a distinct person in the same account. |
| [Maraha](./maraha/notes.md) | Anne's sister in Emmerich's tree, with a daughter and two sons. |
| [Rhoda](./rhoda/notes.md) | Emerentia's daughter and Mara's mother in Emmerich's tree. |

For diagrams comparing the alternatives, see
[Jesus's maternal line and branches](../categories/jesus-maternal-branches.md).
In ordinary mtDNA inheritance, a mother passes mtDNA to all her children;
sons ordinarily do not pass it on. The people and relationships still need
documentation before any genetic connection can be claimed.

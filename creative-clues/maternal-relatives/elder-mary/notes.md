# Elder Mary: Proposed Sister of Anne

In the [secondary quotation of a Byzantine family tree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sobe_(sister_of_Saint_Anne)),
Mary wife of Matthan is the mother of three daughters: elder **Mary**, Sobe,
and Anne. The elder Mary is said to have a daughter, **Salome the midwife**.

```text
Mary wife of Matthan
├── elder Mary → Salome the midwife
└── Anne → Mary mother of Jesus → Jesus
```

If biological, Salome and Jesus would ordinarily inherit mtDNA from the
shared maternal ancestor. The primary Greek and the identification of this
Salome need checking. Do not equate her automatically with
[Mary Salome, mother of the Sons of Thunder](../mary-salome/notes.md).
The account does not establish children of this midwife.

# Maraha: Proposed Sister of Anne

In [Emmerich's visionary account](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/emmerich/lifemary.v.html),
Maraha is a younger daughter of [Ismeria](../ismeria/notes.md) and sister
of Anne and Sobe. Her children are an **unnamed daughter** and two sons,
**Arastaria and Cocharia**, described as disciples.

If these relationships were biological, all three children would ordinarily
receive Maraha's mtDNA. Her daughter could pass it to children; the text
does not identify that daughter's children in the cited passage. Her sons
would not ordinarily pass their mtDNA on.

# Creative Clues: The History Case

This is a workspace for exploring questions about Jesus, earlier traditions,
and how stories and teachings may have changed over time. Treat "hijacked" as
a question to investigate, not an established conclusion.

## Start here

- [Case map](./case-map.md): the investigation, findings, and open leads.
- [Starter leads](./categories/starter-leads.md): possible paths into the case.
- [Charmed: Gods, Power, and a Family Line](./categories/charmed-gods-and-lineage.md):
  a fictional and mythic pattern to explore.
- [Clue template](./templates/clue.md): record a new observation or question.
- [Songs](./songs/): a separate place for creative songwriting.
- [Archive](./archive/): keep older leads without losing the trail.
- [Gnostic Theology Archive](../gnostic-theology/): a related collection of
  interpretations and sources to investigate, not independently verified evidence.

## How to investigate

1. Write down what caught your attention and why it feels meaningful.
2. Record what can actually be checked: the source, date, and exact passage.
3. Separate what the source says from your interpretation and other explanations.
4. Look for sources that challenge the idea as well as ones that support it.
5. Update the clue when you learn more; an unanswered question is a valid result.

Personal connections and coincidences can be meaningful prompts for reflection.
They do not by themselves establish a historical fact or prove that an event was
intended as a message. Note them as personal interpretations, not evidence.

This is a public repository: avoid recording private details you do not want
others to see.

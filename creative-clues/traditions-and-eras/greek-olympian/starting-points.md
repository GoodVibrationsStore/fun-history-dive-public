# Greek and Olympian: Generations and the Changing of Rule

## One primary family tree

In [Hesiod's *Theogony*](https://www.theoi.com/Text/HesiodTheogony.html),
commonly dated around the late eighth or early seventh century BCE, Chaos
appears first, followed by Earth (Gaia) and other primordial beings. Gaia
brings forth Heaven (Uranus); Gaia and Uranus have children including
Cronus and Rhea. Cronus and Rhea are parents of Zeus and his siblings.

```text
Gaia → Uranus
Gaia + Uranus → Cronus and Rhea
Cronus + Rhea → Hestia, Demeter, Hera, Hades, Poseidon, Zeus
```

In this narrative Cronus challenges his father and Zeus later overcomes
Cronus and the Titans. This is a story about succession and divine rule, not
a genetic history of actual families. It is also not a timeline in which
Greek gods historically appeared only after Egyptian or Levantine worship.

## Clues and cautions

- **Threatened child:** Cronus's fear and treatment of his children make a
  useful comparison with the *Charmed* Gideon/Wyatt story. Similar structure
  does not establish historical identity or a hidden ancient lineage.
- **Sisters and stars:** The Pleiades are a separate Greek mythic and
  astronomical lead; the [Pleiades investigation](../../categories/pleiades-and-mythic-overlays.md)
  records how lists and interpretations vary. They are not automatically
  the same as a modern human family.
- **Cronus and Rhea:** Hesiod makes them siblings and parents together, but
  does not call them twins. Rhea's protection of Zeus is a mythic
  hidden-child comparison, not evidence of a human maternal line.
- **Weekday and planetary names:** The
  [planet-and-archon comparison](./planets-weekdays-and-archons.md) separates
  attested weekday names from the chat's unsupported fixed ruler chart.
- **Cross-cultural influence:** Scholars compare Greek succession motifs
  with Near Eastern material; see [this review of Scully's study](https://bmcr.brynmawr.edu/2016/2016.05.25).
  Comparison and historical contact do not prove every similar god is the
  same being.

# Early Christian: Family, Wisdom, and the Hidden Light

## Keep the sources separate

- [Luke 1](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke%201%3A5-7%2C36-45&version=NRSVUE)
  calls Mary and Elizabeth relatives without specifying their exact
  relationship. It does not identify their maternal grandmother.
- The second-century [*Protevangelium of James*](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0847.htm)
  names Anna and Joachim as Mary's parents. Its account is not proof of an
  uninterrupted first-century pedigree.
- The [*Apocryphon of John*](https://earlychristianwritings.com/text/apocryphonjohn.html)
  presents the Invisible Spirit, Barbelo, Sophia, and Yaldabaoth in a
  particular revelatory narrative. It is not a family history of Mary.
- [Irenaeus's report](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0103101.htm) of one
  Valentinian scheme pairs Sophia and Theletos. It is a hostile second-century
  outsider's account, and the name must not be inserted into other texts
  without evidence.

## Why this matters for the case

The *Apocryphon* describes a luminous helper hidden in Adam from the
rulers—an ancient hidden-light motif. The shared chat extends this into a
secret maternal mtDNA conduit, but that biological claim is not in the text.
Barbelo and Sophia are distinct in that account, and neither is identified
as Emorun.
